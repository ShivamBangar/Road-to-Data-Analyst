Q.) Weather Observation Station 16

Query the smallest Northern Latitude (LAT_N) from STATION that is greater than 38.7780.
Round your answer to 4 decimal places.

ANSWER:-

select cast(min(LAT_N) as decimal (10,4))
from station
where LAT_N > 38.7780


Q.)Weather Observation Station 17

Query the Western Longitude (LONG_W)where the smallest Northern
Latitude (LAT_N) in STATION is greater than 38.7780 .
Round your answer to 4 decimal places.

ANSWER:-

SELECT cast (LONG_W as decimal (10,4))
FROM STATION
WHERE LAT_N = (select min(LAT_N) from STATION where LAT_N > 38.7780)
